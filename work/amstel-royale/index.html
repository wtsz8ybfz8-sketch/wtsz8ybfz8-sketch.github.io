Amstel Royale — Taste Success

BrandAmstel Royale
CampaignTaste Success
ContextM&C Saatchi Abel
My roleSocial Media Manager
MarketBurundi

01 — The challenge

After the 2015 elections, Burundi faced a sharp drop in forex, making Heineken hard to import. Amstel Royale was created as a premium beer sold only in Burundi, but its perceived high price kept people from drinking it regularly.

02 — The objective

Position Amstel Royale as an aspirational premium brand and invite people to “Taste Success”, tying the beer to elevated lifestyles, quality brewing and moments of celebration.

03 — What I did

  1. 01Developed a digital content framework that aligned awareness, intrinsic and extrinsic messaging with the campaign objectives.
  2. 02Identified platform-specific activation opportunities to reach the target audience where they were.
  3. 03Designed and delivered a series of hero and supporting content pieces, keeping the brand consistent across touchpoints.
  4. 04Led audience engagement through user-generated content, polls and influencer collaborations to reinforce the brand’s premium cues.

04 — Outcome

The campaign positioned Amstel Royale as a brand synonymous with sophistication and premium quality. By matching the messaging to Burundians’ own aspirations, it made choosing Amstel Royale a statement of refined taste and success.
