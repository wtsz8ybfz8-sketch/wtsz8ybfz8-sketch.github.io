Yoco — Built on Small

BrandYoco
CampaignBuilt on Small
ContextYoco (in-house)
My roleSocial Media Intern
MarketSouth Africa

01 — The challenge

Yoco launched Built on Small to spotlight the often-overlooked “small economy” thriving outside South Africa’s big cities: the businesses quietly driving the country’s broader economy.

02 — The objective

Show, through intimate portraits and personal stories, the resilience, innovation and community spirit that make small-town businesses essential, and reinforce Yoco’s mission to empower them.

03 — What I did

  1. 01End-to-end content creation and management for the campaign.
  2. 02Crafted entrepreneur profiles for social.
  3. 03Sourced photography.
  4. 04Managed social media distribution.
  5. 05Fostered community engagement around the featured stories.

04 — Outcome

85%of comments were positive

Consistent publishing and scheduling took the featured businesses beyond the campaign website. People shared their own experiences with the business owners, which deepened the emotional connection and extended the campaign’s reach.
